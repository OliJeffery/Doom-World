# The Ogryn

You are big. Bigger than most men. Bigger than most things that want to kill you. You are strong, too. Strong enough to pull a damaged Chimera across a battlefield, to move mountains if the little 'uns ask you to. Strong enough to carry your wounded friend to safety while being shot at by thousands of enemy troops, your body a shield against the endless rain of death. Strong enough that you don't feel pain like the little 'uns do, your flesh hardened by the perpetual conflict for survival.

You aren't smart. You know that. The little 'uns are smart. They tell you what to do, and you do it. Because they are your friends. Because they keep you safe. Because when the horrors of the deep jungle emerge, when the abominations that hunger for human flesh crawl from the shadows, you are the one who stands between them and your friends. And nothing gets past you. Nothing. You are the wall, the barrier, the unbreakable shield that protects the little 'uns from the shadows.

Protect your friends. Follow orders. Be strong. That is what you do. That is who you are. That is your duty to the Emperor, and you won't fail.

## Names

*Ogryn*: Nork, Grug, Thud, Bonk, Smash, Crush, Biggun, Stomp, Grunt, Bork

## Look

Choose one for each:

Simple Eyes, Confused Eyes, or Loyal Eyes

Shaved Head, Messy Hair, or Helmet

Huge Body, Massive Body, or Bulky Body

Scarred Skin, Tattooed Skin, or Marked Skin

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 12+Toughness.

When you attack with a Ripper Gun in melee or ranged combat, add +1d8 damage.

## Starting Moves

You are Ogryn, so you get this move:

### Ogryn

When you**follow orders from someone you trust**, you may use Toughness instead of Intelligence or Perception/Willpower for that action.

You start with these moves:

### Break Through

When you**use pure strength to destroy an inanimate obstacle**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, choose 3. ✴On a 7-9 choose 2.

- It doesn't take a very long time
- Nothing of value is damaged
- It doesn't make an inordinate amount of noise
- You can fix the thing again without a lot of effort

### Thick Hide

You ignore the clumsy tag on armor you wear. You also have +1 armor from your natural toughness.

### Bodyguard

Choose someone you protect. When you**stand in defense of that person**, roll+Toughness. ✴On a 10+, hold 3. ✴On a 7–9, hold 1. So long as you stand in defense, when you or the person you protect is attacked you may spend hold, 1 for 1, to choose an option:

- Redirect an attack from the person you protect to yourself
- Halve the attack's effect or damage
- Open up the attacker to an ally giving that ally Advantage against the attacker
- Deal damage to the attacker equal to your level

### Simple Mind

When you**try to understand something complicated**, roll+Intelligence. ✴On a 10+, you see through the complexity to the core truth. You understand what really matters, what's actually happening, without getting lost in details. The GM will give you a clear, practical answer that cuts through deception and confusion. ✴On a 7–9, you grasp the basics—the simple, essential truth. You understand enough to act, though you might miss some nuance. The GM will tell you what you understand and what you might have missed. ✴On a 6-, you don't understand it at all, but you think you do, and you're confident in your misunderstanding.

### Survival

### Duty

## Gear

Your load is 15+Strength. You carry rations (5 uses, 1 weight). Choose your defenses:

- Flak armor (1 armor, 1 weight) and field gear (1 weight)
- Carapace armor (2 armor, 3 weight)

Choose your weapon:

- Ripper gun (near, +1 damage, 2 weight)
- Heavy stubber (near, far, +1 damage, 3 weight)
- Brute shield (+1 armor, 2 weight) and club (close, forceful, 1 weight)

Choose one:

- Extra rations (5 uses, 1 weight)
- Medkit (0 weight)
- Field gear (1 weight)

## Advanced Moves

When you gain a level from 2-5, choose from these moves.

### Merciless

When you deal damage, deal +1d4 damage.

### Loyal

When you**protect a squadmate**, you take Advantage to all actions related to that protection.

### Iron Hide

You gain +1 armor.

### Unstoppable

When you**power through an obstacle or enemy**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, you break through completely. ✴On a 7-9, you break through but take damage or leave yourself exposed, your choice.

### Simple Solutions

When you**try to solve a problem in the simplest way possible**, roll+Toughness. ✴On a 10+, your simple solution works perfectly. ✴On a 7-9, your solution works but has unexpected consequences.

### Protective Rage

When someone you protect is hurt, you deal +1d6 damage until the threat is eliminated.

### Multiclass Dabbler

Get one move from another specialization. Treat your level as one lower for choosing the move.

### Thick Skull

You have +1 armor against attacks to your head.

### Carry

When you**carry someone or something heavy**, you can move at normal speed and still fight. You ignore the weight of anything you're carrying.

When you gain a level from 6-10, choose from these moves or the level 2-5 moves.

### Bloodthirsty

Replaces: Merciless

When you deal damage, deal +1d8 damage.

### Perfect Loyalty

Replaces: Loyal

When you**protect a squadmate**, you take Advantage to all actions related to that protection and they take +1 armor.

### Steel Hide

Replaces: Iron Hide

You gain +2 armor.

### Unstoppable Force

Replaces: Unstoppable

When you**power through an obstacle or enemy**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, you break through completely and deal damage to anything in your way. ✴On a 7-9, you break through but take damage or leave yourself exposed, your choice.

### Perfect Solutions

Replaces: Simple Solutions

When you**try to solve a problem in the simplest way possible**, roll+Toughness. ✴On a 10+, your simple solution works perfectly and you gain Advantage. ✴On a 7-9, your solution works but has unexpected consequences.

### Protective Fury

Replaces: Protective Rage

When someone you protect is hurt, you deal +1d8 damage and take Advantage until the threat is eliminated.

### Multiclass Initiate

Requires: Multiclass Dabbler

Get one move from another specialization. Treat your level as one lower for choosing the move.

### Thick Skull Master

Replaces: Thick Skull

You have +2 armor against attacks to your head and are immune to stun damage.

### Carry Everything

Replaces: Carry

When you**carry someone or something heavy**, you can move at normal speed and still fight. You ignore the weight of anything you're carrying. You can carry multiple people or objects at once.

### Nork's Strength

When you**use your strength to accomplish something impossible**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, you do it. ✴On a 7-9, you do it but suffer a debility or take damage, your choice. ✴On a 6-, you fail but you don't give up—you can try again immediately.

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you become an unstoppable wall one last time, a barrier between your friends and all the horrors of Catachan. You may stand between your friends and all danger, or charge into the enemy with impossible, world-breaking strength. Roll+Toughness. ✴On a 10+, you create an absolutely impassable barrier or destroy a major threat completely. All allies take +3 armor, Advantage, and are immune to all damage from the threat you face. Your final act protects them completely—no enemy can reach your friends while you stand, and even after you fall, your presence lingers, granting all allies +1 armor for the rest of the mission. Your name is remembered by the little 'uns, and they tell stories of the big friend who never gave up. Future Ogryns who know your story take Advantage when protecting their friends. ✴On a 7-9, you protect all allies or destroy a major threat. They take +2 armor and Advantage. No enemy can reach your friends while you stand. ✴On a miss, your final roar echoes across the battlefield, shaking the very ground. Your friends are completely safe, and your sacrifice ensures their escape. All allies take +2 armor, Advantage, and deal +1d4 damage for the rest of the battle. Your name is remembered by the little 'uns, and they tell stories of the big friend who never gave up. Your legend grants all Ogryns Advantage when they are at death's door.
