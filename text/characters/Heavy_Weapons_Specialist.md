# The Heavy Weapons Specialist

When something big comes out of the jungle, something that won't die to a lasgun, something that shouldn't exist, that is when they call for you. You carry the heavy weapons, the instruments of the Emperor's wrath made manifest. The flamers that burn the jungle back, purging the darkness with holy fire. The heavy bolters that can punch through chitin and scale, that can turn the mightiest xenos into paste. The weapons that end threats that lesser arms can't touch.

You aren't fast. You aren't quiet. But when the Catachan Devils come, when the giant scorpions attack, when something that shouldn't exist tries to eat your squad, you are the one who makes it regret that decision. You are the wall of fire and lead that stands between your squad and the things that want to kill them, the barrier that the darkness can't pass, the instrument of divine judgment that brings death to the enemies of mankind.

Lock and load. Make every shot count. The jungle is full of targets, and the Emperor demands their destruction.

## Names

*Catachan*: Blast, Cannon, Hammer, Lex, Max, Rex, Shell, Thunder, Wex, Vex

## Look

Choose one for each:

Hard Eyes, Dead Eyes, or Watchful Eyes

Helmet, Shorn Hair, or Bandana

Heavy Weapons Harness or Standard Gear

Built Body, Bulky Body, or Scarred Body

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 10+Toughness.

When you attack with a heavy weapon, add +1d8 damage.

## Starting Moves

You are Catachan, so you get this move.

### Catachan

When you**survey the battlefield, even for a moment, and ask, "What here is the biggest threat?"**the GM will tell you, honestly.

You start with these moves:

### Suppressing Fire

When you**lay down covering fire to protect an ally**, roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+ you grant them +1 armor or remove one threat. ✴On a 7–9, they are protected, but you draw the threat to yourself.

### Armored

You ignore the clumsy tag on armor you wear.

### Heavy Weapons Expertise

You have mastered the art of heavy weapons warfare. When you**set up a heavy weapon in a prepared position**, you gain +1 to all attacks with that weapon until you move. Additionally, when you attack with a heavy weapon, you may choose to spend extra ammo before rolling. For each point of ammo spent, you may choose an extra target or deal +1d4 damage to your primary target.

### Unstoppable Barrage

When you**lay down continuous fire with your heavy weapon**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, you suppress all enemies in the area—they cannot advance and must take cover or suffer your damage. ✴On a 7-9, you suppress enemies in the area, but your weapon begins to overheat—you must stop firing next turn or risk a jam. ✴On a miss, your weapon jams or runs out of ammo at a critical moment.

## Duty

Choose a duty:

### Duty

Eliminate a high-value target with heavy weapons.

### Survival

Endanger yourself to protect someone weaker than you with covering fire.

### Combat

Destroy a threat that normal weapons can't harm.

## Gear

Your load is 12+Strength. You start with rations (5 uses, 1 weight), carapace armor (2 armor, 3 weight), and your heavy weapon. Choose your secondary weapon:

- Lascarbine (near, 1 weight)
- Laspistol (near, 1 weight) and Catachan knife (close, 1 weight)
- Chainsword (close, 2 weight)

Choose one:

- Field gear (1 weight)
- Rations (1 weight) and medkit (0 weight)
- Extra ammo (3 ammo, 1 weight)

## Advanced Moves

When you gain a level from 2-5, choose from these moves.

### Merciless

When you deal damage, deal +1d4 damage.

### Area Denial

When you**lay down suppressing fire in an area**, enemies must press on to enter that area. ✴On a 10+, they can't enter at all. ✴On a 7-9, they can enter but take damage.

### Armor Mastery

When you**make your armor take the brunt of damage dealt to you**, the damage is negated but you must reduce the armor value of your armor (your choice) by 1. The value is reduced each time you make this choice. If the reduction leaves the item with 0 armor it is destroyed.

### Weapon Specialization

Choose one type of heavy weapon (Heavy Bolter, Flamer, Grenade Launcher, Multi-Melta, or Autocannon). When you use that weapon type, you deal +1d4 damage and ignore the reload tag.

### Target Priority

When you read the signs during combat, you take +1.

### Sustained Fire

When you**keep firing at the same target**, your next attack against that target deals +1d4 damage.

### Multiclass Dabbler

Get one move from another specialization. Treat your level as one lower for choosing the move.

### Iron Hide

You gain +1 armor.

### Master of Destruction

When you have access to a tech-priest or armory you can modify your heavy weapon. Choose one: add +1 damage, add +2 piercing, or add the area effect tag. This modification is permanent.

When you gain a level from 6-10, choose from these moves or the level 2-5 moves.

### Bloodthirsty

Replaces: Merciless

When you deal damage, deal +1d8 damage.

### Kill Zone

Replaces: Area Denial

When you**lay down suppressing fire in an area**, enemies must press on to enter that area. ✴On a 10+, they can't enter at all and take damage if they try. ✴On a 7-9, they can enter but take damage.

### Armored Perfection

Replaces: Armor Mastery

When you**choose to let your armor take the brunt of damage dealt to you**, the damage is negated and you take Advantage against the attacker, but you must reduce the armor value of your armor (your choice) by 1. The value is reduced each time you make this choice. If the reduction leaves the item with 0 armor it is destroyed.

### Perfect Target

Replaces: Target Priority

When you read the signs during combat, you take +2 and can ask an additional question.

### Sustained Barrage

Replaces: Sustained Fire

When you**keep firing at the same target**, your next attack against that target deals +1d8 damage.

### Multiclass Initiate

Requires: Multiclass Dabbler

Get one move from another specialization. Treat your level as one lower for choosing the move.

### Steel Hide

Replaces: Iron Hide

You gain +2 armor.

### Master of Destruction

Replaces: Master of Destruction

When you have access to a tech-priest or armory you can modify your heavy weapon. Choose two: add +1 damage, add +2 piercing, or add the area effect tag. These modifications are permanent. You may take this move multiple times, choosing different modifications each time.

### Devastating Impact

When you**strike a target with overwhelming firepower**, roll+Strength. ✴On a 10+, you deal your damage and the target is knocked back, disoriented, or forced into cover—they take Disadvantage on their next action. ✴On a 7-9, you deal your damage and the target is staggered—they take Disadvantage on their next attack. ✴On a miss, your heavy weapon's recoil throws off your aim and you take Disadvantage on your next attack.

### Eye for Targets

When you look over an enemy's defenses, ask the GM how much armor they have.

### Superior Firepower

When you use Ranged Combat on a 12+ you deal your damage, avoid their attack, and suppress, dismay, or frighten your enemy.

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you unleash everything your heavy weapon has left in a final, apocalyptic barrage. You may target every enemy in sight with devastating firepower. Roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+, you deal your damage+3d12 to all enemies in sight, ignoring all armor. The area becomes a permanent kill zone—enemies must press on with Disadvantage to enter it, and those who fail take your damage+1d10. All allies take Advantage and +2 armor for the rest of the battle. Your final barrage creates a legend—the battlefield is permanently scarred, and future enemies who hear of it take Disadvantage. ✴On a 7-9, you deal your damage+2d10 to all enemies, ignoring armor. The area becomes a kill zone for the rest of the battle. All allies take Advantage and +1 armor. ✴On a miss, your weapon overheats and explodes in a cataclysmic blast, dealing your damage+2d10 to all nearby enemies and creating a permanent barrier of fire and wreckage that blocks all movement. Your final stand becomes a legend, and your squad takes +2 forward and +1 armor for the rest of the battle. Your sacrifice ensures the mission succeeds.
