# The Sergeant

You have been fighting on Catachan longer than most of your squad has been alive. You have seen horrors that would break lesser men. The ambushes that come from nowhere. The traps that claim the unwary. The things that crawl out of the dark, things that shouldn't exist, things that hunger for human flesh. You have lost more friends than you can count, their names etched into your memory alongside their final screams. You bear more scars than you have clean skin, each one a testament to a death you cheated, a horror you survived. And you are still here, still fighting, still serving the Emperor's will.

Experience is the only teacher that matters on a Death World, and you have learned every lesson in blood. You have learned the hard way what works and what gets you killed, what tactics save lives and what strategies lead to mass graves. Your squad looks to you for wisdom, for leadership, for the knowledge that only comes from surviving when you should have died a hundred times over. You have forgotten more about jungle warfare than most men will ever learn, and what you remember is written in the scars of the fallen.

Pass on what you know. Keep them alive. That is your duty, your burden, your sacred charge. Fail, and their blood is on your hands for all eternity.

## Names

*Catachan*: Arden, Greiss, Iron, Jackson, Ripper, Rock, Sly, Stone, Straken, Zane

## Look

Choose one for each:

Hard Eyes, Dead Eyes, or Watchful Eyes

Peaked Cap, Shorn Hair, or Bandana

Sergeant's Insignia or Standard Gear

Built Body, Scarred Body, or Ravaged Body

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 10+Toughness.

When you attack with any weapon, add +1d4 damage.

## Starting Moves

You are Catachan, so you get this move.

### Catachan

When you**survey the battlefield, even for a moment, and ask, "What here is the biggest threat?"**the GM will tell you, honestly.

You start with these moves:

### Lead by Example

When you**take action to inspire and protect your squad**, roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+ you grant all squad members Advantage on their next action or remove one threat facing the squad. ✴On a 7–9, they are inspired and gain Advantage, but you draw the threat to yourself.

### Tactical Coordination

When you**give orders to coordinate your squad's actions**, you may use the Give Orders move with Advantage. Additionally, when you successfully give an order, you may choose an additional benefit from the Give Orders move list.

### Squad Discipline

When you**maintain a formation with your squad**, you may use the Maintain Formation move with Advantage. Additionally, when the formation holds, all squad members gain +1 armor in addition to the formation's normal benefits.

### Armored

You ignore the clumsy tag on armor you wear.

### Mission Command

When you**dedicate yourself to a mission through planning and leadership**, state what you set out to do:

- Eliminate _______, a great threat to the mission
- Defend _______ from the threats that beset them
- Discover the truth of _______

Then choose up to two boons:

- An unwavering sense of direction to _______.
- Resistance to _______ (e.g., fire, poison, disease, etc.)
- A mark of leadership
- Senses that pierce deception
- A voice that commands respect
- A freedom from fatigue and exhaustion

The GM will then tell you what requirement or requirements is needed of you to maintain your command:

- Honor (forbidden: cowardly tactics and tricks)
- Discipline (forbidden: breaking formation or orders)
- Duty (required: observance of mission objectives)
- Valor (forbidden: suffering a threat to live)
- Truth (forbidden: lies)
- Loyalty (required: support to those in need, no matter who they are)

## Duty

Choose a duty:

### Duty

Lead your squad to complete a mission objective.

### Survival

Endanger yourself to protect someone weaker than you.

## Gear

Your load is 12+Strength. You start with rations (5 uses, 1 weight), carapace armor (2 armor, 3 weight), and some mark of your rank, describe it (0 weight). Choose your weapon:

- Lascarbine (near, far, 1 weight) and chainsword (close, 1 weight)
- Lascarbine (near, far, 1 weight) and power sword (close, 1 weight)

Choose one:

- Field gear (1 weight)
- Rations (1 weight) and medkit (0 weight)

## Advanced Moves

When you gain a level from 2–5, choose from these moves.

### Veteran Knowledge

You've learned from experience. Choose one specialization move. You can use that move as if it was a sergeant move. When you select this move, treat yourself as that specialization of level 1. Every time you gain a level thereafter, increase your effective specialization level by 1.

### Take the Hit

When you take damage you can grit your teeth and accept the blow. If you do you take no damage but instead suffer a debility of your choice. If you already have all six debilities you can't use this move.

### Mission Focus

While on a mission you deal +1d4 damage.

### No Retreat

When you**speak aloud your promise to defeat an enemy**, you deal +2d4 damage against that enemy and -4 damage against anyone else. This effect lasts until the enemy is defeated. If you fail to defeat the enemy or give up the fight, you can admit your failure, but the effect continues until you find a way to redeem yourself.

### Charge!

When you**lead the charge into combat**, all squad members who follow gain Advantage on their first attack and +1 armor for the duration of the charge.

### Staunch Defender

When you cover fire you always get +1 hold, even on a 6-.

### Setup Strike

When you close combat, choose an ally. Their next attack against your target does +1d4 damage.

### Mission Protection

You get +1 armor while on a mission.

### Squad Support

When you**coordinate two or more squad members to work together**, roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+, they both gain Advantage and may act in perfect coordination—their actions complement each other flawlessly. ✴On a 7–9, they gain Advantage but must choose one: they're slightly out of sync, or one of them is exposed.

### Field Medic

When you treat an ally's wounds, you heal +1d8 damage.

When you gain a level from 6–10, choose from these moves or the level 2–5 moves.

### Master Tactician

Requires: Veteran Knowledge

When you**see enemy tactics as they happen**, you can ask the GM what their strategy is and its effects. Take +1 when acting on the answers.

### Mission Master

Replaces: Mission Focus

While on a mission you deal +1d8 damage.

### Ever Onward

Replaces: Charge!

When you lead the charge into combat, all squad members who follow gain Advantage on their first attack, +2 armor for the duration of the charge, and may move without triggering danger from the enemies you're charging.

### Impervious Defender

Replaces: Staunch Defender

When you cover fire you always get +1 hold, even on a 6-. When you get a 12+ to cover fire instead of getting hold the nearest attacking creature is stymied giving you a clear advantage, the GM will describe it.

### Tandem Strike

Replaces: Setup Strike

When you close combat, choose an ally. Their next attack against your target does +1d4 damage and they take Advantage against them.

### Mission Armor

Replaces: Mission Protection

You get +2 armor while on a mission.

### Battlefield Command

Replaces: Squad Support

When you**coordinate three or more squad members**, roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+, they all gain Advantage and act in perfect coordination. Additionally, choose one: they all gain +1 armor, they all deal +1d4 damage on their next attack, or they establish or change formation without needing a separate roll. ✴On a 7–9, they gain Advantage but one member is exposed or out of position.

### Perfect Medic

Replaces: Field Medic

When you treat an ally's wounds, you heal +2d8 damage.

### Indomitable

When you suffer a debility (even through Take the Hit) take Advantage against whatever caused it.

### Perfect Leader

When you mission command you choose three boons instead of two. Additionally, when you use Lead by Example, you may grant benefits to all squad members instead of just one.

### Squad Unity

When you**spend time training or coordinating with your squad**, you may increase Squad Cohesion by 1 (maximum 3) at the end of the session, even if you didn't mark a shared experience. This represents your leadership building the squad into a true unit.

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you give one final order that will be remembered forever. Your squad sees your sacrifice and is inspired beyond all measure. Roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+, your final command grants all allies Advantage, +3 armor, and they may reroll any two dice for the rest of the battle. They deal +1d6 damage with all attacks. Your death becomes a rallying cry—they can't fail while your memory drives them. The mission succeeds automatically. Your name becomes a legend, and future sergeants who know your story can invoke your memory once per mission to grant their squad Advantage. ✴On a 7-9, your final command grants all allies Advantage, +2 armor, and they may reroll one die for the rest of the battle. They deal +1d4 damage. ✴On a miss, your final words become a legend that echoes across Catachan. Your squad completes the mission in your name, and your sacrifice ensures their success. All allies take Advantage, +1 armor, and deal +1d4 damage for the rest of the battle. Your name is remembered, and future squads take inspiration from your example—they gain Advantage when they invoke your memory.
