# The Scout

You move like a wraith through the killing ground, a specter of death that the jungle itself can't track. While the others crash through the undergrowth with all the subtlety of a charging grox, you glide ahead, reading the signs of death, spotting the ambushes before they spring. You have spent more time alone in the deep jungle than most men have spent drawing breath, your soul merged with the darkness itself. You know which plants will poison you, which creatures are hunting you, and which shadows conceal the things that shouldn't exist.

Your squad's lives depend on you. Without your eyes, they would walk blindly into a Catachan Devil's nest and be torn apart. Without your ears, they would miss the sound of something massive and hungry moving in the canopy above. Without your instincts, they would all be dead, their bones picked clean by the jungle's endless hunger. You're the one who finds the paths that don't lead to death, the water that isn't tainted, the high ground that offers a moment's respite. You are the one who keeps them alive, for now.

Keep moving. Keep watching. The jungle never sleeps, and neither can you.

## Names

*Catachan*: Ghost, Hunter, Jax, Kael, Shadow, Silent, Stalker, Tracker, Whisper, Wraith

## Look

Choose one for each:

Sharp Eyes, Watchful Eyes, or Hard Eyes

Bandana, Shorn Hair, or Camouflage Paint

Tattered Fatigues, Camouflage Gear, or Light Armor

Lithe Body, Wiry Body, or Scarred Body

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 8+Toughness.

When you attack with a ranged weapon, add +1d4 damage.

## Starting Moves

Choose a regiment and gain the corresponding move:

### Unseen Lurkers

When you navigate the jungle through wilderness whatever job you take you succeed as if you rolled a 10+.

### Green Devils

When you bivouac in the jungle, you don't need to consume a ration.

You start with these moves:

### Hunt and Track

When you**follow a trail of clues left behind by passing creatures**, roll+Perception. ✴On a 7+, you follow the creature's trail until there's a significant change in its direction or mode of travel. ✴On a 10+, you also choose 1:

- Gain a useful bit of information about your quarry, the GM will tell you what
- Determine what caused the trail to end

### Called Shot

When you**attack a defenseless or surprised enemy at range**, you can choose to deal your damage or name your target and roll+Ballistic Skill.

- Head ✴10+: As 7–9, plus your damage ✴7-9: They do nothing but stand and drool for a few moments.
- Arms ✴10+: As 7-9, plus your damage ✴7-9: They drop anything they're holding.
- Legs ✴10+: As 7-9, plus your damage ✴7-9: They're hobbled and slow moving.

### Jungle Sense

You have an almost supernatural connection with the jungle. You can read its signs, feel its dangers, and know its secrets. Choose a focus:

Tracking, Survival, Navigation, Stealth, Threat Detection

Choose a base:

- Alertness +2, Instinct +1, +1 to Read the Signs
- Alertness +2, Instinct +2, Advantage to Press On
- Alertness +1, Instinct +2, +1 to Read the Signs
- Alertness +3, Instinct +1, Advantage to Hunt and Track

Choose as many strengths as your alertness:

Fast, tireless, adaptable, quick reflexes, camouflage, keen senses, stealthy, silent movement

Your jungle sense is trained for specific situations. Choose as many additional trainings as your instinct:

Track, search, scout, guard, identify threats, navigate, survive, evade

Choose as many weaknesses as your instinct:

Paranoid, jumpy, slow, forgetful, stubborn, reckless

### Trust Your Instincts

When you work with your jungle sense on something it's trained in…

- …and you attack the same target, add your alertness to your damage
- …and you track, add your instinct to your roll
- …and you take damage, add your alertness to your armor
- …and you read the signs, add your instinct to your roll
- …and you negotiate, add your instinct to your roll
- …and someone interferes with you, add your alertness to their roll

## Gear

Your load is 11+Strength. You start with rations (5 uses, 1 weight), flak armor (1 armor, 1 weight), and a lascarbine power pack (3 ammo, 1 weight). Choose your armament:

- Lascarbine (near, far, 1 weight) and Catachan knife (close, 1 weight)
- Lascarbine (near, far, 1 weight) and machete (reach, 1 weight)

Choose one:

- Field gear (1 weight) and rations (1 weight)
- Field gear (1 weight) and lascarbine power pack (3 ammo, 1 weight)

## Advanced Moves

You may take this move only if it is your first advancement.

### Jungle Born

You were born and raised in the deepest parts of the Catachan jungle. You gain the Unseen Lurkers starting move if you took the Green Devils one at character creation or vice versa.

When you gain a level from 2-5, choose from these moves.

### Jungle Whisperer

You can read the signs of the jungle so well that you can predict threats before they appear. When you read the signs about jungle threats, you may ask an additional question.

### Familiar Threat

When you use jungle knowledge about a Catachan creature you use Perception instead of Intelligence.

### Dual Strike

When you strike an enemy with two weapons at once, add an extra 1d4 damage for your off-hand strike.

### Camouflage

When you**keep still in natural surroundings**, enemies never spot you until you make a movement.

### Take the Hit

When you**use your jungle sense to take a blow that was meant for an ally**, the damage is negated but your alertness becomes 0. If your alertness is already 0 you can't use this ability. When you have a few hours of rest your alertness returns to normal.

### Suppressing Fire

When you use Ranged Combat you may spend extra ammo before rolling. For each point of ammo spent you may choose an extra target. Roll once and apply damage to all targets.

### Well-Trained

Choose another training for your jungle sense.

### Veteran Scout

You've been scouting the jungle for so long that you've learned to read it like a book. When you navigate the jungle, you can take two roles. You make a separate roll for each.

### A Safe Place

When you**set the watch for the night**, everyone takes +1 to stand watch.

When you gain a level from 6-10, choose from these moves or the level 2-5 moves.

### Jungle Master

Replaces: Jungle Whisperer

You can read the signs of the jungle so well that you can predict threats before they appear. When you read the signs about jungle threats, you may ask two additional questions.

### Hunter's Prey

Replaces: Familiar Threat

When you use jungle knowledge about a Catachan creature you use Perception instead of Intelligence. On a 12+, in addition to the normal effects, you get to ask the GM any one question about the subject.

### Dual Strike Master

Replaces: Dual Strike

When you strike an enemy with two weapons at once, add an extra 1d8 damage for your off-hand strike.

### Weak Point

When you know your target's weakest point your shots have 2 piercing.

### Pathfinder

Replaces: Veteran Scout

When you navigate the jungle you can take two roles. Roll twice and use the better result for both roles.

### A Safer Place

Replaces: A Safe Place

When you set the watch for the night everyone takes Advantage to stand watch. After a night in bivouac when you set the watch everyone takes Advantage.

### Observant

When you hunt and track, on a hit you may also ask one question about the creature you are tracking from the read the signs list for free.

### Special Technique

Choose a move from another specialization. So long as you are working with your jungle sense you have access to that move.

### Unnatural Instinct

Your jungle sense has become so attuned that you can sense things that shouldn't be possible. Describe it. Give yourself +2 alertness and +1 instinct, plus a new training.

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you become one with the jungle one last time, merging your spirit with Catachan itself. You may lead your enemies into a devastating trap, reveal a perfect hidden path to safety, or mark the location of every threat for your allies. Roll+Perception. ✴On a 10+, the jungle itself rises to aid you. You create a perfect escape route or trap that saves all allies and eliminates a major threat. All allies take Advantage, +2 armor, and enemies take Disadvantage for the rest of the battle. The jungle remembers you—your allies can read the signs to learn about all threats in the area for the rest of the mission. Your spirit becomes part of Catachan, a permanent guide. Future scouts who know your story take Advantage when navigating the jungle. ✴On a 7-9, you create an escape route or trap that helps all allies or hinders all enemies. Allies take Advantage and +1 armor, enemies take Disadvantage. ✴On a miss, your final act becomes a warning carved into the jungle itself—all allies can read the signs to learn about all threats in the area for the rest of the mission. They take +2 forward and +1 armor. Your spirit becomes part of Catachan, a guide for those who know how to listen. The jungle itself protects those you cared about.
