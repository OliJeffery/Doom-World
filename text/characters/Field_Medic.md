# The Field Medic

On Catachan, death comes in ten thousand forms. Poison that dissolves flesh from bone. Disease that rots the mind before it claims the body. Wounds that fester and refuse to heal, turning proud Guardsmen into screaming wrecks. You have watched men die from mere scratches, their bodies consumed by infection in hours. You have watched men survive wounds that should have killed them instantly, their faith in the Emperor keeping them alive long enough for your ministrations. You know which plants can save a life and which will end it in agony. You know how to treat a man who has been stung by a death-spider, bitten by a venom-snake, or infected with the jungle rot that eats away at the soul.

Your squad trusts you with their lives, for you are all that stands between them and the Emperor's will. When they are bleeding out, when the poison courses through their veins, when the fever burns them alive from within, you are the one who saves them. You are the one who keeps them fighting when their bodies scream for the release of death. You are the difference between life and death, between service and failure, between honor and disgrace.

Save who you can. Learn from the ones you can't. The jungle teaches harsh lessons, and failure means another soul lost to the darkness.

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 8+Toughness.

When you attack with a lascarbine or laspistol, add +1d4 damage.

## Starting Moves

Choose a regiment and gain the corresponding move:

### Green Devils

You are one with the jungle's remedies. When you treat wounds you are also granted a special version of Field Treatment as a rote which only works on your regiment.

### Savage Jaguars

Your medical knowledge is extensive. Choose one specialization move. You can use that move as if it was a medic move.

You start with these moves:

### Medical Specialization

You've trained in combat medicine and field surgery. Choose your area of specialization:

- Trauma Surgery
- Toxicology
- Field Triage
- Emergency Medicine
- Jungle Medicine
- Combat Wound Care

Choose your medical approach:

- Fast and efficient—you prioritize speed, add Technique: Rapid Treatment
- Thorough and careful—you prioritize effectiveness, add Technique: Precise Treatment
- Battlefield hardened—you prioritize keeping soldiers in the fight, add Technique: Combat Medicine
- Resourceful—you make do with limited supplies, add Technique: Improvised Treatment

### Field Treatment

When you**apply field treatment using your medical specialization**, you provide practical medical aid related to your area of expertise. The GM will tell you what effect your treatment has.

### Stabilize

When you**use your medkit and medical training to stabilize a critically wounded patient**, roll+Intelligence. ✴On a 7+, so long as you continue treatment and use medical supplies, you can keep them alive and prevent them from dying. ✴On a 10+, you successfully stabilize them and they can act normally, though severe wounds may still cause problems. If they take aggressive action or further damage, they may destabilize again.

### Prepare Supplies

When you**spend uninterrupted time (an hour or so) preparing and organizing your medical supplies**, you:

- Restock your medical supplies.
- Prepare specialized treatments of your choice whose total levels don't exceed your own level+1, and none of which is a higher level than your own level.
- Restock all of your standard supplies, which never count against your limit.

### Treat Wound

When you**use specialized medical supplies you've prepared**, roll+Intelligence. ✴On a 10+, the treatment is successfully applied and you have enough supplies left to use them again. ✴On a 7–9, the treatment is applied, but choose one:

- You draw unwelcome attention or put yourself in a spot. The GM will tell you how.
- Your treatment is rushed or incomplete—take Disadvantage to treat wounds until the next time you prepare supplies.
- You use up the specialized supplies. You can't use them again until you prepare supplies and restock them.

Note that maintaining treatments with ongoing effects will sometimes cause a penalty to your roll to treat wounds.

## Advanced Moves

When you gain a level from 2–5, choose from these moves.

### Specialized Training

Choose one medical treatment. You can prepare that treatment as if it was one level lower.

### Invigorate

When you**heal someone**they take Advantage to their damage.

### The Scales of Life and Death

When**someone is at death's door in your presence**, they take Advantage to the roll.

### Steady Hands

When you treat wounds you ignore the first -1 penalty from ongoing treatments.

### First Aid

Basic field treatment is a standard procedure for you, and therefore doesn't count against your limit of granted treatments.

### Emergency Response

When you prepare supplies you get 1 hold and lose any hold you already had. Spend that hold when you or an ally takes damage to immediately intervene with appropriate treatment (a quick injection, a pressure bandage, a stimulant) and negate the damage.

### Self-Sacrifice

When you**take damage to save another**, you may take +1d4 damage (ignoring armor). If you do, take Advantage to treat wounds.

### Empower

When you treat wounds, on a 10+ you have the option of choosing from the 7–9 list. If you do, you may choose one of these effects as well:

- The treatment's effects are doubled
- The treatment's targets are doubled

### Medical Diagnosis

When you**examine a patient and assess their condition**, your medical training reveals the best course of treatment. If you follow that course, mark experience.

### Field Protection

When you wear no armor you get 2 armor from your medical gear and equipment.

### Expert Medic

When you heal someone else of damage, add your level to the amount of damage healed.

When you gain a level from 6–10, choose from these moves or the level 2–5 moves.

### Master Medic

Requires: Specialized Training

Choose one medical treatment in addition to the one you picked for specialized training. You can prepare that treatment as if it was one level lower.

### Jungle Doctor

The first time you**spend time studying jungle remedies as appropriate to your specialization**after taking this move, choose a feature associated with your medical training (enhanced senses, steady hands, perfect memory, etc.). When you emerge from study, you permanently gain that physical feature.

### Aftermath

When you**take time after a conflict to treat the wounded and assess casualties**, take Advantage.

### Expert Technique

Replaces: Steady Hands

You ignore the -1 penalty from two treatments you maintain.

### Greater First Aid

Requires: First Aid

Advanced field treatment is a standard procedure for you, and therefore doesn't count against your limit of granted treatments.

### Emergency Mastery

Replaces: Emergency Response

When you prepare supplies you gain 2 hold and lose any hold you already had. Spend that hold when you or an ally takes damage to immediately intervene with appropriate treatment (a quick injection, a pressure bandage, a stimulant) and negate the damage.

### Martyr

Replaces: Self-Sacrifice

When you**take damage to save another**, you may take +1d4 damage (ignoring armor). If you do, take Advantage to treat wounds and add your level to any damage done or healed by the treatment.

### Field Armor

Replaces: Field Protection

When you wear no armor you get 3 armor from your medical gear.

### Greater Empower

Replaces: Empower

When you treat wounds, on a 10–11 you have the option of choosing from the 7–9 list. If you do, you may choose one of these effects as well. On a 12+ you get to choose one of these effects for free.

- The treatment's effects are doubled
- The treatment's targets are doubled

### Multiclass Dabbler

Get one move from another specialization. Treat your level as one lower for choosing the move.

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you perform one final act of medical heroism, using every stimulant, every treatment, every technique you know to save your squad. You push your body beyond its limits, administering emergency treatments to everyone. Roll+Willpower. ✴On a 10+, you heal all allies to full Wounds, remove all debilities, grant them Advantage, +2 armor, and immunity to all diseases and poisons for the rest of the mission. Your final act ensures they survive. Your medical techniques are passed on—all allies gain a permanent Advantage to treat wounds, and one ally gains your full medical knowledge, becoming a Field Medic in your memory. Your name is remembered in medicae bays across Catachan, and your techniques save countless lives. Future medics who know your story take Advantage when stabilizing the dying. ✴On a 7-9, you heal all allies for 3d8 Wounds, remove all debilities, and grant them Advantage and +1 armor. They are immune to disease and poison for the rest of the battle. ✴On a miss, your final act becomes legendary. All allies are healed to full Wounds, all debilities are removed, and they take +2 forward and +1 armor. Your sacrifice ensures no one else dies today. Your name is remembered in medicae bays across Catachan, and your techniques save countless lives. Your legend grants all future medics +1 forward when they are at death's door.
