# The Sniper

You kill from the shadows, a specter of death that strikes without warning, without mercy, without hesitation. One shot. One kill. That is your way, your creed, your sacred duty to the Emperor. While the others are fighting in the open, you are in the canopy, in the rocks, in the perfect position where death can't find you. You have learned the patience of the grave. You have learned to wait, to watch, to strike when the moment is perfect. You have learned that the best way to survive is to never be seen at all, to be a ghost that the jungle itself can't track.

Your lascarbine is an extension of your will, a tool of divine judgment. You know every creak, every whisper, every way it can deliver death. You have learned to read the wind, the light, the distance, to calculate the perfect moment when your target's life can be extinguished. You have learned to kill things that most men can't even see, to end lives from impossible distances, to be the instrument of the Emperor's wrath made manifest in a single, perfect shot.

Take the shot. Make it count. The jungle is watching, and so is the Emperor.

## Names

*Catachan*: Bolt, Cade, Crosshair, Dex, Longshot, Marbo, Marksman, Striker, Trigger, Vex

## Look

Choose one for each:

Watchful Eyes or Dead Eyes

Bandana, Shorn Hair, or Camouflage Paint

Camouflage Gear, Tattered Fatigues, or Light Armor

Lithe Body, Wiry Body, or Scarred Body

## Stats

Your maximum Wounds is 6+Toughness.

When you attack with a sniper rifle or long-las, add +1d6 damage.

## Starting Moves

Choose a regiment and gain the corresponding move:

### Unseen Lurkers

When you attack with a ranged weapon, deal +2 damage.

### Green Devils

You are a professional. When you use jungle knowledge or read the signs about threats, take +1.

You start with these moves:

### Threat Assessment

When you**spend a moment to survey a dangerous area**, roll+Perception. ✴On a 10+, hold 3. ✴On a 7–9, hold 1. Spend your hold as you move through the area to ask these questions:

- Is there a threat here and if so, what activates it?
- What does the threat do when activated?
- What else is hidden here?

### Marksman

When you**attack a target at range**, roll+Ballistic Skill. ✴On a 10+, you have a perfect shot—deal your damage. ✴On a 7–9, you still hit, but the GM will offer you two options between suspicion, danger, or cost.

### Precision Shot

When you**attack a surprised or defenseless enemy at range**, you can choose to deal your damage or name your target and roll+Ballistic Skill. ✴On a 10+ choose two. ✴On a 7–9 choose one.

- You don't reveal your position
- You deal your damage+1d6
- You create an advantage, Advantage to you or an ally acting on it
- Reduce their armor by 1 until they repair it

### Ghost

When someone tries to detect your position you can tell them any position you like.

### One Shot, One Kill

You've mastered the art of the perfect shot. Choose a specialty from the list below; that specialty gives you +1 damage when used. You also start with three uses of specialized ammo for that specialty. Whenever you have time to craft and a safe place to work you can make three uses of specialized ammo for free.

- Armor Piercing: Your shots ignore 2 armor
- Explosive: Your shots deal +1d4 damage in an area
- Incendiary: Your shots set targets on fire
- Hollow Point: Your shots deal +1d6 damage to unarmored targets

## Gear

Your load is 9+Strength. You start with rations (5 uses, 1 weight), flak armor (1 armor, 1 weight), 3 uses of your chosen specialized ammo, and 10 thrones. Choose your arms:

- Catachan knife (hand, 1 weight) and lascarbine (near, 1 weight)
- Sniper rifle (far, precise, 2 weight)

Choose a ranged weapon:

- 3 throwing knives (thrown, near, 0 weight)
- Lascarbine (near, far, 2 weight) and power pack (3 ammo, 1 weight)

Choose one:

- Field gear (1 weight)
- Medkit (0 weight)

## Advanced Moves

When you gain a level from 2–5, choose from these moves.

### Precise Shot

When using a precise or ranged weapon, your precision shot deals an extra +1d6 damage.

### Cautious

When you use threat assessment you always get +1 hold, even on a 6-.

### Reputation

When you**make a show of your kill count or marksmanship**, choose someone present. They will do anything they can to avoid being your target.

### Shoot First

You're never caught by surprise. When an enemy would get the drop on you, you get to act first instead.

### Ammo Master

After you've used a specialized ammo type once it's no longer dangerous for you to use.

### Specialized Load

You can load even complex specialized ammo quickly. When you load specialized ammo that's not dangerous for you to use, it takes no extra time.

### Ammo Crafting

When you have time to gather materials and a safe place to work you can create three rounds of any one specialized ammo you've used before.

### Hard to Hit

When you're in cover, you have +1 armor.

### Connections

When you put out word to other snipers or scouts about something you want or need, roll+Leadership. ✴On a 10+, someone has it, just for you. ✴On a 7–9, you'll have to settle for something close or it comes with strings attached, your call.

When you gain a level from 6–10, choose from these moves or the level 2–5 moves.

### Master Marksman

Replaces: Precise Shot

When using a precise or ranged weapon, your precision shot deals an extra +1d8 damage and all other attacks deal +1d4 damage.

### Extremely Cautious

Replaces: Cautious

When you use threat assessment you always get +1 hold, even on a 6-. On a 12+ you get 3 hold and the next time you come near a threat the GM will immediately tell you what it does, what triggers it, who set it, and how you can use it to your advantage.

### Master Craftsman

Replaces: Ammo Crafting

When you have time to gather materials and a safe place to work you can create three rounds of any specialized ammo you've used before. Alternately you can describe the effects of specialized ammo you'd like to create. The GM will tell you that you can create it, but with one or more caveats:

- It will only work under specific circumstances
- The best you can manage is a weaker version
- It'll take a while to take effect
- It'll have obvious side effects

### Harder to Hit

Replaces: Hard to Hit

You have +1 armor. When you're in cover, you have +2 armor instead.

### Ghost

When you press on on a 12+, you transcend the danger. You not only do what you set out to, but the GM will offer you a better outcome, true beauty, or a moment of grace.

### Perfect Shot

You can make impossible shots. When you use Ranged Combat, you can choose to ignore cover, range, or other obstacles. A perfect shot costs extra ammo; you can never choose to reduce ammo on a 7–9.

### Escape Route

When you're in too deep and need a way out, name your escape route and roll+Agility. ✴On a 10+ you're gone. ✴On a 7–9 you can stay or go, but if you go it costs you: leave something behind or take something with you, the GM will tell you what.

### Camouflage

When you have time and materials you can create camouflage that will fool anyone into thinking you're part of the environment. Your actions can give you away but your appearance won't.

### Assassination

When you take time to make a plan to eliminate a target, name the target you want to kill and ask the GM these questions. When acting on the answers you and your allies take Advantage.

- Who will notice they're dead?
- What's their most powerful defense?
- Who will come after you?
- Who else wants them dead?

### Blaze of Glory

When you**choose to go out in a blaze of glory at death's door**, you take the perfect shot—the one that will be remembered forever. Name your target and roll+Ballistic Skill. ✴On a 10+, your shot is impossible, legendary, transcendent. You deal your damage+3d12, ignore all armor, cover, and defenses. The target dies instantly, and your shot passes through up to five additional enemies in a perfect line, dealing your damage+2d10 to each, ignoring armor. All allies take Advantage and may ask the GM one question about any enemy or threat. Your final shot becomes a story told around campfires—a ghost in the jungle that never misses. Future snipers who hear your legend take Advantage on their first shot of each battle. ✴On a 7-9, you deal your damage+2d10, ignore armor, and the target dies. Your shot passes through up to two additional enemies, dealing your damage+1d10 to each. All allies take Advantage. ✴On a miss, your shot finds an unexpected target or creates a perfect opening—your allies all take +2 forward, +1 armor, and may ask the GM two questions about the battlefield. Your final shot becomes a story told around campfires, a ghost in the jungle that never misses. The enemy never sees it coming.
