# The Ruins

Jungle Reclaimed	Solitary, Amorphous, Stealthy

Crushing vines (d10+2 damage)	17 Wounds	2 Armor

Close, Reach

**Special Qualities:**Plant, Camouflage

Abandoned Imperial structures quickly become overgrown with the jungle. Sometimes, the jungle itself takes on a malevolent intelligence, using the ruins as a trap. Vines snake through corridors, roots crack foundations, and the entire structure becomes a death trap. The jungle is always hungry, and abandoned buildings are just another meal.*Instinct*: To consume

- Collapse structures
- Entangle in vines
- Use ruins as a trap

Rust-Mite Swarm	Horde, Tiny, Swarming

Corrosive bite (d4 damage ignores armor)	1 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Swarming, Corrosive

Tiny insects that feed on metal. They can strip a lasgun to scrap in minutes, and they're attracted to the heat and energy of Imperial technology. They nest in abandoned structures, swarming anything that enters. Their corrosive saliva dissolves metal, making them a nightmare for anyone carrying equipment.*Instinct*: To consume metal

- Swarm equipment
- Dissolve metal
- Nest in structures

Ghost of the Fallen	Solitary, Terrifying, Intelligent

Psychic touch (d10 damage ignores armor)	14 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Incorporeal, Warp-Touched

The spirits of Guardsmen who died in the ruins, bound to the place of their death by trauma and the Warp. They're not truly alive or dead, existing in a state of perpetual agony. They attack the living out of jealousy and rage, their touch draining life force. Some say they're trying to warn others away, others claim they're simply mad.*Instinct*: To torment

- Phase through walls
- Drain life force
- Manifest as terrifying visions

Automated Defense System	Solitary, Intelligent, Organized

Twin-linked lascannon (d12+2 damage, ignores armor)	16 Wounds	4 Armor

Near, Far, Forceful

**Special Qualities:**Construct, Automated

Ancient defense systems that still function long after their creators are gone. They're programmed to attack anything that doesn't match their IFF codes, and after centuries of isolation, that means everything. They're heavily armored and armed with devastating weapons, making them nearly impossible to destroy.*Instinct*: To defend

- Lock onto targets
- Fire devastating weapons
- Call for reinforcements

Mutant Infestation	Horde, Small, Swarming

Claw (d6 damage)	3 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Warp-Touched, Regenerating

The descendants of those who took refuge in the ruins, mutated by exposure to Warp energy and the toxic environment. They're barely human, twisted into horrific forms by generations of mutation. They're feral and aggressive, attacking anything that enters their territory. Some retain fragments of their former intelligence, using it to set traps and ambushes.*Instinct*: To survive

- Swarm in numbers
- Set traps
- Use knowledge of the ruins

Structural Collapse	Solitary, Forceful

Crushing debris (d12+3 damage, ignores armor)	20 Wounds	5 Armor

Reach, Forceful, Messy

**Special Qualities:**Environmental, Unstoppable

The ruins themselves are unstable, weakened by years of jungle growth and neglect. A single misstep can trigger a collapse that buries everything beneath tons of debris. The jungle has worked its way into the foundations, and the entire structure is a death trap waiting to happen.*Instinct*: To collapse

- Trigger structural failure
- Bury in debris
- Create new hazards

Servitor Remnant	Group, Intelligent, Organized

Mechanical claw (d8 damage)	9 Wounds	2 Armor

Close, Reach

**Special Qualities:**Construct, Automated

The remains of servitors that were left behind when the outpost was abandoned. Their programming has degraded over the years, leaving them with only fragments of their original purpose. They're still functional, but their behavior is erratic and unpredictable. Some attack anything that moves, others continue their original tasks mindlessly. They're a reminder of what was lost, and a warning of what might happen to those who stay too long.*Instinct*: To serve

- Continue degraded programming
- Attack perceived threats
- Use knowledge of the ruins

Warp-Tainted Structure	Solitary, Terrifying, Warp-Touched

Reality warp (d10 damage ignores armor, area)	18 Wounds	0 Armor

Near, Far, Forceful

**Special Qualities:**Warp-Touched, Unstable

A structure that has been corrupted by exposure to the Warp. Reality itself is unstable within its walls, warping and shifting unpredictably. The laws of physics don't apply here—corridors lead to impossible places, gravity shifts direction, and time flows strangely. Those who enter are often lost forever, trapped in a nightmare of shifting reality.*Instinct*: To corrupt

- Warp reality
- Trap victims in impossible spaces
- Spread corruption

Scavenger Pack	Horde, Small, Intelligent, Organized

Improvised weapon (d6 damage)	4 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Intelligent, Organized

Desperate survivors who have taken refuge in the ruins. They're not necessarily hostile, but they're desperate and dangerous. They've learned to survive in the ruins, using their knowledge to set traps and ambushes. They're scavengers, stripping anything of value from the ruins. They might be willing to trade, but they're just as likely to attack anyone who looks like they have something worth taking.*Instinct*: To survive

- Set traps and ambushes
- Use knowledge of the ruins
- Strike from hiding
