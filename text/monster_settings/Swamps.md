# The Swamps

Rot-Worm	Group, Large, Poisonous

Acidic bite (d10 damage ignores armor)	12 Wounds	2 Armor

Close, Reach

**Special Qualities:**Amphibious, Regenerating

Massive worms that dwell in the murky swamps. Their bite secretes a corrosive acid that dissolves flesh and metal. They can regenerate lost segments, making them nearly impossible to kill. They hunt by sensing vibrations in the water and mud, striking from below with devastating force.*Instinct*: To consume

- Strike from below
- Dissolve with acid
- Regenerate lost segments

Bog-Ghoul	Horde, Small, Stealthy

Claw (d6 damage)	4 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Amphibious, Camouflage

Humanoid creatures that have adapted to life in the swamps. They're covered in mud and algae, making them nearly invisible until they strike. They hunt in packs, dragging victims into the murky water to drown. Some say they're mutated humans, others claim they're a separate species entirely. Either way, they're deadly.*Instinct*: To drown

- Strike from hiding
- Drag prey into the water
- Swarm in numbers

Acid-Slime	Solitary, Amorphous, Poisonous

Corrosive touch (d10 damage ignores armor)	18 Wounds	1 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Amorphous, Acidic

A massive blob of acidic slime that oozes through the swamps. It dissolves everything it touches—flesh, metal, even rock. It moves slowly but inexorably, leaving a trail of destruction in its wake. Fire is the only thing that can truly harm it, but getting close enough to use it is suicide.*Instinct*: To dissolve

- Ooze into armor or equipment
- Dissolve everything it touches
- Split into smaller blobs

Swamp-Crawler	Group, Large, Stealthy

Pincer (d8+2 damage, 2 piercing)	11 Wounds	3 Armor

Close, Reach

**Special Qualities:**Amphibious, Camouflage

Massive crustaceans that lurk in the swamp waters. Their shells are nearly impenetrable, and their pincers can crush bone. They're ambush predators, waiting motionless in the murk until prey comes close. They can hold their breath for hours, making them perfect ambush hunters.*Instinct*: To ambush

- Strike from the water
- Crush with powerful pincers
- Retreat into the murk

Miasma Cloud	Solitary, Amorphous, Poisonous

Toxic breath (d8 damage ignores armor, area)	15 Wounds	0 Armor

Near, Far

**Special Qualities:**Amorphous, Poisonous

A living cloud of toxic gas that drifts through the swamps. It's drawn to heat and movement, slowly enveloping anything that catches its attention. The toxins dissolve lungs and flesh, leaving only bones behind. It's nearly impossible to fight—the only option is to run.*Instinct*: To poison

- Envelop prey in toxic cloud
- Dissolve flesh and organs
- Drift toward heat sources

Leech-Beast	Horde, Small, Swarming

Blood drain (d4 damage ignores armor)	2 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Amphibious, Regenerating

Massive leeches that hunt in swarms. They attach to prey and drain blood at an alarming rate. A single leech is annoying—a swarm can drain a man dry in minutes. They're attracted to open wounds and can sense blood from kilometers away.*Instinct*: To drain

- Attach to prey
- Drain blood rapidly
- Swarm in overwhelming numbers

Quicksand Pit	Solitary, Amorphous, Stealthy

Drowning (d10 damage ignores armor)	15 Wounds	0 Armor

Close, Reach

**Special Qualities:**Amorphous, Environmental

Not all dangers in the swamp are alive. Some are the swamp itself. Quicksand pits look like solid ground until weight is applied, at which point they become a death trap. The more you struggle, the deeper you sink. Some say the swamp itself is alive, creating these traps to feed. Either way, once you're caught, escape is nearly impossible without help.*Instinct*: To consume

- Entrap the unwary
- Pull deeper into the muck
- Drown in mud and water

Bog-Wraith	Solitary, Terrifying, Intelligent, Warp-Touched

Chilling touch (d10 damage ignores armor)	16 Wounds	0 Armor

Close

**Special Qualities:**Incorporeal, Warp-Touched, Poisonous

The spirits of those who died in the swamps, bound to the place of their death by the Warp and their own despair. They appear as misty figures rising from the bog, their touch freezing flesh and draining life. They're drawn to the living, jealous of their warmth and life. Some say they're trying to warn others away, but most just want company in their eternal suffering.*Instinct*: To torment

- Rise from the bog
- Freeze with chilling touch
- Drain life force

Swamp-Giant	Solitary, Huge, Forceful

Crushing fist (d12+3 damage, ignores armor)	21 Wounds	3 Armor

Close, Reach, Forceful

**Special Qualities:**Amphibious, Regenerating

A massive humanoid creature that has adapted to life in the swamps. It's covered in mud and algae, making it nearly invisible until it rises from the water. It's incredibly strong, capable of crushing a Chimera with its bare hands. It's not particularly intelligent, but it's cunning enough to use the swamp to its advantage. It's territorial, attacking anything that enters its domain.*Instinct*: To dominate

- Rise from the swamp
- Crush with overwhelming force
- Use the swamp as a weapon
