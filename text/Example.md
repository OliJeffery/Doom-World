# Example of Play

Oli is GMing a game with Dan (playing Marcus, a Close Combat Specialist), Rob (playing Jax, a Scout), Tom (playing Kael, a Sniper), and Kris (playing Doc, a Field Medic). The squad has been sent to investigate Outpost Gamma-7, which went dark three weeks ago, its silence a testament to the endless horror that defines existence on Catachan. They have found the outpost overgrown with the jungle, consumed by the darkness, and signs of a struggle—but no bodies, for the jungle is always hungry, and it never leaves evidence of its victims.

As they approach the main structure, three venom-snakes drop from the canopy above, their scales shifting to match the jungle around them, their eyes burning with hunger. Two more coil around the rusted remains of a Chimera, ready to strike, their fangs dripping with poison. A pack of mutants—barely human, twisted by Warp exposure, their souls corrupted by the darkness—emerge from the overgrown barracks, brandishing crude weapons, their eyes empty of all but the hunger for human flesh. The jungle itself seems to be closing in, vines creeping across the path behind them, sealing them in, trapping them in the endless darkness.

Once I describe the situation, I give them a chance to act: "What do you do?"

Tom speaks first. "Is there enough cover around the Chimera wreck for a firing position?" "Yes," I say, "the jungle has grown over it, but there's enough metal and foliage to break up your silhouette. The mutants haven't spotted you—they're focused on the main entrance." "Kael drops to one knee, shouldering his long-las. I take a shot at the lead mutant, center mass. I want to drop him before they realize we're here."

I assess the situation. "They're not expecting you—they're caught off guard. Deal your damage." Tom rolls damage and gets 6. "The mutant leader drops, a smoking hole through his chest. The others scatter, taking cover. The venom-snakes have noticed the commotion. They're coming your way."

Dan steps forward. "I'm not letting those snakes reach Kael. Marcus revs his chainsword and charges, putting himself between the snakes and the sniper."

"Close combat. Roll+Weapon Skill."

"Rolling with Strength. I got an 8."

"You deal your damage to the first snake. Roll it."

Dan rolls. "8 damage with my chainsword."

"The chainsword tears through the snake. The other two strike—one gets past your guard. Take 4 damage from the venom. The poison burns, but your flak armor absorbs most of it."

Kris responds. "Doc sees Marcus take that hit and moves to help. I use Field Treatment—get that venom neutralized before it spreads."

"Roll+Willpower for Field Treatment."

Kris rolls. "That's a 9. I can stabilize him and prevent the poison from spreading, but I'll need to use medical supplies."

"You inject Marcus with an antitoxin from your medkit. The burning stops, the wound closes. You're down one use of medical supplies, but Marcus is back in the fight. Rob, what's Jax doing?"

Rob considers. "Jax is reading the situation. I want to know what the mutants are really up to—are they just scavengers, or is there something else?"

"Read the Signs. Roll+Perception."

"Rolling with Willpower. That's a 7."

"You notice something important: the mutants aren't just looting. They're setting up some kind of device—looks like a crude vox-caster, but it's been modified. There are Chaos symbols carved into it. And you can see movement in the command center—something's in there, and it's not human. What should you be on the lookout for?"

Rob considers. "I need to know if there are more threats we haven't seen yet. Are there other entrances to the outpost, or are we looking at the only way in?"

"You can see two other ways in: a collapsed section of wall on the east side, and what looks like a maintenance hatch on the south. But more importantly, you notice the vines around the outpost are moving. Not just swaying in the wind—actually moving, like they're alive. The jungle itself is trying to seal you in. What are you doing about that?"

Tom speaks up. "Kael's been watching the command center. I want to get a better look at what's inside. Can I make out any details through the windows?"

"The windows are mostly covered in vines and grime, but you can see flickering light inside—not the steady glow of Imperial lumens, but something more erratic. Purple-tinged. Warp-light. And you catch a glimpse of something moving: it's got too many limbs, and they're in the wrong places. What are you doing about it?"

Dan responds. "Marcus isn't waiting. I rev the chainsword and charge the remaining mutants."

"Close combat. Roll+Weapon Skill."

Dan rolls. "That's a 10. I deal my damage and avoid their attack."

"The chainsword tears through the first mutant like paper. The second one tries to bring his crude axe down on you, but you sidestep and the chainsword finds his throat. Two down. But the thing in the command center—it's noticed you. The door bursts open, and something that used to be human steps out. It's got tentacles where its arms should be, and its face is a mass of eyes. It's a Chaos Spawn. What do you do?"

Kris responds. "Doc sees that thing and knows we're in trouble. I'm going to use Prepare Supplies—I want to get a stim-pack ready for Marcus, because I have a feeling he's going to need it."

"Roll+Willpower."

Kris rolls. "That's an 11. I prepare a stim-pack and a dose of combat stims. I can use them when needed."

"You've got the supplies ready. The Chaos Spawn lurches forward, its tentacles writhing. Rob, Jax is in the best position to see what's happening. What are you doing?"

Rob thinks. "Jax is going to use his Jungle Tracker ability. I want to find a way to flank this thing, or maybe find something in the environment I can use against it."

"Roll+Willpower."

Rob rolls. "That's a 9. I can track it, but there's a complication."

"You notice the Spawn's movements are erratic—it's leaving a trail of Warp corruption behind it. But you also see that the vines are reacting to it, pulling away. The jungle doesn't like Warp energy. You could use that. However, you also notice the mutants weren't alone—there are more of them inside the outpost, and they're coming out now. What do you do?"

Tom acts. "Kael lines up a shot on the Spawn. I'm going for a headshot—if it has a head. I want to put it down before it reaches Marcus."

"Roll+Ballistic Skill for your headshot move."

Tom rolls. "That's a 7. I deal my damage, but there's a cost."

"The shot hits, dealing 8 damage. The Spawn staggers, but it's not down. And your position is compromised—one of those venom-snakes has found you. It's coiled around a branch above you, ready to strike. What are you doing about that?"

Dan responds. "Marcus sees Kael in trouble and the Spawn closing. I use Unstoppable Advance—I charge through everything, chainsword roaring, to get between that snake and Kael."

"Roll+Weapon Skill."

Dan rolls. "That's a 10. I push through."

"You barrel through, the chainsword cutting through vines and undergrowth. The Spawn's tentacles reach for you, but you're moving too fast. You get between Kael and the snake just as it strikes—the chainsword meets fang, and the snake is torn apart. But the Spawn is right behind you. What are you doing?"

Kris responds. "Doc sees Marcus is about to be overwhelmed. I throw him the stim-pack I prepared."

"Marcus, you catch the stim-pack. You can use it now to heal 1d8+1 Wounds, or save it. The Spawn is closing in. What are you doing?"

Dan decides. "Marcus jabs the stim-pack into his leg and revs the chainsword. Time to finish this."

"The stims take effect—you heal 6 Wounds. The chainsword roars, and you charge the Spawn. What are you doing?"

Dan rolls for close combat. "That's an 11. I'm going all out—maximum damage."

"The chainsword tears into the Spawn, and this time it finds something vital. The creature collapses, its form dissolving into Warp energy. But as it dies, you hear a voice—not from the Spawn, but from inside the outpost. 'You are too late. The ritual is complete.' What do you do?"
