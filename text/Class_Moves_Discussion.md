# Close Combat Specialist Moves

## Chainsword

The base description you choose is just a description. Choosing an eviscerator doesn't automatically give you Reach range, for example. You could choose an eviscerator as the description, then Close as the range. Your chainsword is something special, or your technique with it is different, just describe why your weapon has the tags you've chosen.

## Flak-Hardened

Armor that is reduced to 0 armor is effectively destroyed. You'll need to get new armor assigned, which may require Respect or finding salvage.

# Scout Moves

## Jungle Tracker

Your bonuses only apply when your familiar is doing something it's trained in. A creature not trained to attack enemies won't be any help when you're attacking a Catachan Devil.

# Sniper Moves

## Headshot

Reducing armor until they repair it means that they lose armor until they do something that compensates for your damage. If you're fighting an armored Guardsman that might mean a fresh suit of flak armor, but for a thick-hided Catachan Devil it's until they've had time to heal up (or protect the wound you left).

## Special Ammunition

In order to make more doses of your chosen toxin you need to be reasonably able to gather the required materials. If you're locked up in a cell you're not going to be able to get the materials you need.

# Field Medic Moves

## Field Treatment

Your medical knowledge applies to treating wounds, stabilizing patients, and using medical supplies. The exact effects depend on the situation and available resources.

# Sergeant Moves

## Lead by Example

Your leadership bonuses apply when you're coordinating your squad. The exact effects depend on the tactical situation and your squad's capabilities.
